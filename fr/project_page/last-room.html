LAST ROOM

This work presents the worldview of an unknown man before the collapse of all things, his last spiritual refuge, a chaotic virtual reality world. I use glitch as the language of this universe. I work with 360-degree videos, which I export as digital files. I modify the digital values ​​to create the glitch effect, then integrate them into the virtual reality world. Next, I process the textures of 3D objects using glitches. It also includes blurry videos that I filmed and that have gradually eroded through glitching, as well as numerous 3D models with hand-drawn, pixelated animation textures. What I want to show is a spiritual refuge. Perhaps this is our way of inhabiting an age of decline, using error and manual labor as means of expression.
